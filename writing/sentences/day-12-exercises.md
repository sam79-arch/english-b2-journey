## 📚 Core Grammar Reminder

| Form | Usage | Signal Words | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Duration from past to now (still ongoing) | for X hours, since, how long, all day | *I have been working for 3 hours* |
| **Present Perfect Simple** | Completed action OR life experience | already, yet, just, ever, number of times | *I have finished 5 projects* |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Process (emphasis on activity) | for duration, about progress | *How long have you been coding?* |
| **Present Perfect Simple** | Result (what was accomplished) | countable results | *I have written 100 lines of code* |

**1.** "I have been waiting for this moment. / I have waited for this moment."

- Mixed contexts may require both forms to show process + result

## 📋 Summary: Units 9-10 Key Concepts

| Concept | Present Perfect Continuous | Present Perfect Simple |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Process, Duration, Activity | Result, Completion, Achievement |
| **Form** | have/has + been + -ing | have/has + past participle |
| **Time signals** | for X hours, since, all day, how long, today | already, yet, just, ever, number/quantity |
| **Example** | "I have been coding for 3 hours" | "I have written 50 lines of code" |
| **Use** | Still ongoing, emphasis on activity | Completed, what was accomplished |
