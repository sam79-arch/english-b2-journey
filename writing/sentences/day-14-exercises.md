## Q1 — The number of... (Easy)

The number of students in the class _____ increased significantly this semester.

a) have  
b) **is**  
c) are  
d) were

**Explanation:** "The number of..." = singular subject. Even though "students" is plural, the subject is "number" (singular concept).

---

## Q2 — A number of... (Easy)

A number of applications _____ already received by our HR department.

a) has  
b) is  
c) **have been**  
d) was

**Explanation:** "A number of..." = plural subject. "Applications" is plural, and "a number of" indicates multiple items.

The number of errors in the project report _____ much higher than expected.

## Q5 — Either...or (Tricky)

Either the manager or the team members _____ responsible for submitting the quarterly report.

a) is  
b) **are**  
c) am  
d) has been

**Explanation:** With Either...or, the verb agrees with the noun closest to it. "Members" (plural) is closest, so "are."

---

## Q6 — Neither...nor (Tricky)

Neither the CEO nor her assistants _____ informed about the server outage until this morning.

a) was  
b) **were**  
c) has been  
d) is

**Explanation:** With Neither...nor, verb agrees with the nearest noun. "Assistants" (plural) is nearest, so "were."

## Q7 — Police Exception (Exception Rule)

The police _____ investigating the data breach that occurred last week.

a) is  
b) **are**  
c) was  
d) have been

**Explanation:** "Police" is always plural in English, even though it looks singular. Always use plural verb.

## PARAGRAPH 1: Project Kickoff Meeting

The number of team members assigned to the new mobile app project are growing every week. We have been building the architecture for three months already, and I am thinking we have found a solid foundation. Since last Monday, the design team have been implementing the new UI framework properly. A number of challenges have emerged, including compatibility issues that we are having difficulty solving.

---

**📋 ANSWER KEY — PARAGRAPH 1**

| Error # | Location | Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---------|----------|-------|---------|-----|
| **1 (SVA)** | "The number of team members...***are*** growing" | **are** | **is** | "The number of..." = singular subject (the concept of "number"), NOT plural. Use singular "**is**" |
| **2 (Tense)** | "We have been building...for three months ***already***" | **have been building** | **have built** | "Already" signals COMPLETION (result), not ongoing duration. Change to Perfect Simple "**have built**" |
| **3 (Stative)** | "I ***am thinking*** we have found a solid foundation" | **am thinking** | **think** | "Think" (= opinion/believe) is stative verb; NEVER use continuous. Should be "**I think**" |
| **4 (Stative)** | "we ***are having difficulty*** solving issues" | **are having** | **have difficulty** | "Have" (possess/experience) is stative; cannot use continuous form. Change to "**we have difficulty solving**" |

## PARAGRAPH 3: Post-Deployment Incident Report

A number of vulnerabilities have been discovered in the new API, and the security team are working round-the-clock to fix them. Neither the product management nor the development lead are aware of the severity until this morning's briefing. We have been discovering and patching bugs for three weeks and completed over 50 issues already. I am understanding the risks involved, which seems why management wants a 48-hour rollback window.

---

**📋 ANSWER KEY — PARAGRAPH 3**

| Error # | Location | Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---------|----------|-------|---------|-----|
| **1 (SVA)** | "the security team **are** working" | **are** | **is** | "Team" = singular collective noun; verb agrees with "team." Use singular "is" |
| **2 (Tense Mix)** | "have been discovering...for three weeks and **completed** over 50 issues already" | **have been...completed** | **have completed** over 50 issues for result-focused approach | "For three weeks" suggests duration (Perfect Continuous) BUT "completed 50 issues already" emphasizes RESULT. Mixed tenses in same clause = confusing. Choose: either "**have been discovering for 3 weeks**" (duration) OR "**have completed 50 issues**" (result), not both |
| **3 (Stative)** | "I **am understanding** the risks" | **am understanding** | **understand** | "Understand" (= know/realize) is stative verb; NEVER use continuous form. No "am understanding," "is understanding," etc. |
| **4 (SVA: Neither...nor)** | "Neither the product management nor the development lead **are aware**" | **are** | **is** | Rule: With Neither...nor, verb agrees with **nearest noun**. "Lead" (singular, closest to verb) → use singular "**is aware**" |

# PART 3: DIALOGUE WRITING — Model & Analysis

**Scenario:** Two colleagues (A = Dev Lead, B = QA Manager) discussing project progress at a team standup.

**Requirements checked:**
- ✅ Each speaker uses ≥1 Perfect Continuous  
- ✅ ≥1 SVA structure ("a number of," "the team," "either")

---

## Model Dialogue

**A:** "I've been working on integrating the payment module since Monday, and I believe the backend logic is almost finalized."

**B:** "Good. The number of integration tests that need to run is quite high. A number of QA engineers have been writing edge-case tests for the last three days."

**A:** "How many test suites have you completed?"

**B:** "We've completed 12 suites and discovered 8 priority-2 bugs. Neither the staging environment nor the development servers are consistently stable, though."

**A:** "I understand the frustration. Either we extend testing by two days or we launch with known issues and monitor closely. What does the team prefer?"

---

## Grammar Breakdown

| Speaker | Sentence | Grammar Point | Analysis |
|---------|----------|---|---|
| A | "I've **been working** on integrating...since Monday" | Perfect Continuous | Duration from past to now—correct usage ✓ |
| A | "I **believe** the backend logic is almost finalized" | Stative verb | "Believe" (thinking/opinion) cannot be continuous → "believe" alone ✓ |
| B | "The **number** of integration tests...is quite high" | SVA | "The number of" = singular → "is" ✓ |
| B | "**A number** of QA engineers **have been writing**" | SVA + Perfect Continuous | "A number of" = plural → "have"; Perfect Continuous for duration ✓ |
| B | "Neither...nor the development servers **are**...stable" | SVA (Neither...nor) | Verb agrees with nearest: "servers" (plural) → "are" ✓ |
| A | "I **understand** the frustration" | Stative verb | "Understand" (know) is stative → no continuous ✓ |
| A | "Either...or we**launch**...the **team prefer**?" | SVA (Either...or) | "Prefer" with "team" (singular collective) or "team" (plural). Here: "What does the team prefer" = singular collective ✓ |
