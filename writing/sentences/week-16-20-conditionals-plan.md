## 🚨 Common Traps to Watch For

### **Trap 1: "Were" in Conditional 2**
❌ "If I was you..." (common in speech)  
✅ "If I were you..." (always in writing, even "I")  
→ **Rule:** Conditional 2 = always "were" for all subjects (formal English)

### **Trap 2: Mixing Conditional 1 + 2 Signals**
❌ "If I will go tomorrow, I see you" (mixing will)  
✅ "If I go tomorrow, I will see you" (Cond 1: present + will)  
✅ "If I went tomorrow, I would see you" (Cond 2: past + would)  
→ **Rule:** Don't use "will" in If-clause; it goes in result clause

### **Trap 3: "Have had" vs "Had"**
❌ "If I have had more time, I would finish" (wrong time reference)  
✅ "If I had had more time, I would have finished" (Cond 3: had + had)  
→ **Rule:** Conditional 3 = had + V3 (had + past participle)

### **Trap 4: Missing "would" in result**
❌ "If I were rich, I buy a house" (missing would)  
✅ "If I were rich, I would buy a house"  
→ **Rule:** Conditional 2 result = would/might/could + V
