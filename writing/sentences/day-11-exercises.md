## 📚 Core Grammar Reminder

| Form | Usage | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Perfect** | Action finished, result matters NOW (recent, no specific time) | *I have finished my work* |
| **Present Perfect: Experience** | Had experience/visited place (ever? How many times?) | *Have you been to Japan?* |
| **Present Perfect: Duration** | Started in past, continues to now | *I have lived here for 10 years* |
| **Past Simple** | Action finished, specific time mentioned OR no connection to present | *I finished yesterday / I worked there* |

## Key Learning Points from Errors

**Pattern 1: Present Perfect vs Past Simple with Duration** ❌ Q7, Q13, Q14
- ✅ "**for** + time period + **now**" = Present Perfect (has lived)
- ❌ "**yesterday**, **last week**, **specific date**" = Past Simple (completed, updated)
- **Note:** "For 15 years / 8 years now" means the action continues to present → has lived/worked

**Pattern 2: "How many" Questions** ❌ Q5, Q17
- ✅ "How many countries have you visited?" → Present Perfect (cumulative experience)
- ❌ "How many countries did you visit?" → Only correct if there's a specific time context
- **Remember:** "How many" about life experience = Present Perfect

**Pattern 3: Repeated Experience vs Single Moment** ❌ Q1, Q9, Q15
- ✅ "I have finished" = Result matters now (no specific time)
- ✅ "I have been to Korea twice" = Life experience summary
- ❌ "I finished / went" = Implies specific time even when not stated

**Pattern 4: Keywords That Signal Present Perfect** ⚠️
- ✅ Already, yet, ever, twice, many times, just, recently, so far, before
- ❌ Yesterday, last week, last month, in 2020, ago, when X happened

## Weak Areas to Review

1. **Duration signals** - Recognize "for X years/months" as Present Perfect duration marker
2. **Question formation** - "How many" and "How long" questions require Present Perfect for life experience
3. **Result vs timeline** - When no specific time is given, think about present relevance
