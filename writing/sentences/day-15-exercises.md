# 📋 YOUR CHEAT SHEET (Keep This Open!)

## 🎯 The 4 Golden Rules

| Rule | Signal | Tense | Example |
|------|--------|-------|---------|
| **Rule 1** | **For** + duration (still ongoing) | Perfect Continuous | I have **been working** for 3 hours |
| **Rule 2** | Specific time (yesterday, at 5 PM, last week) | Past Simple | I **worked** yesterday |
| **Rule 3** | Two past events, one BEFORE the other | Past Perfect | I **had finished** before he arrived |
| **Rule 4** | Countable result (3 reports, twice, the best) | Perfect Simple | I **have written** 5 emails |

---

## 🟢🟠🟣 Signal Word Groups

| Group | Signal Words | Which Rule? |
|-------|--|---|
| 🟢 **PRESENT** | always / usually / now / right now / just / already / for / since | Rule 1 or 4 |
| 🟠 **PAST** | yesterday / last week / ago / while / when | Rule 2 or 3 |
| 🟣 **PERFECT** | before / by the time / had / for+duration | Rule 1 or 3 |

---

## 🚫 Stative Verb Categories

| Category | Examples | Wrong Form |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Emotions | like, love, hate, prefer, enjoy | ❌ I am liking |
| Thinking | know, think, believe, suppose | ❌ I am knowing |
| Possession | have, own, possess | ❌ I am having (unless = experience) |
| Perception | see, hear, smell, taste, seem, appear | ❌ I am seeing |
| Other | want, need, understand, mean, deserve | ❌ I am wanting |

---

## 🎯 SVA Traps

| Trap | Subject | Verb |
|------|---------|------|
| The number of X | **The number** (singular) | **is/was** |
| A number of X | **Applications** (plural) | **are/were** |
| Either...or | Verb agrees with **NEAREST** noun | is/are |
| Neither...nor | Verb agrees with **NEAREST** noun | is/are |
| One of the X | **One** (singular) | **is/was** |
| Police (exception) | Always plural | **are/were** |

# PART 2 — Stative Verb Trap (8 questions)

**Instructions:**

---

### Q1 — Emotion Verb (like/hate)

Which is correct?

a) "I **am liking** the new project management tool."  
b) "I **like** the new project management tool." ✓

**Correct:** b  
**Category:** Emotion (like)  
**Reason:** "Like" = stative verb (opinion/preference), NOT action. Never use continuous form. ❌ am liking / ✅ like

---

### Q2 — Emotion Verb (hate)

Which is correct?

a) "Our users **are hating** the slow load time."  
b) "Our users **hate** the slow load time." ✓

**Correct:** b  
**Category:** Emotion (hate)  
**Reason:** "Hate" = stative (strong feeling). Use simple form. ❌ are hating / ✅ hate

---

### Q3 — Thinking Verb (know)

Which is correct?

a) "Do you **know** the password for the production server?"  
b) "Are you **knowing** the password for the production server?" ✗

**Correct:** a  
**Category:** Thinking (know)  
**Reason:** "Know" = state of having information (stative). ❌ are knowing / ✅ know

---

### Q4 — Thinking Verb (think = believe)

Which is correct?

a) "I **think** we should deploy on Friday morning." ✅  
b) "I **am thinking** we should deploy on Friday morning." ✗

**Correct:** a  
**Category:** Thinking (think = believe/opinion)  
**Reason:** When "think" = belief/opinion, it's stative. When "think" = consider/focus, it can be continuous ("I'm thinking about..."). Here = opinion → simple form only.

---

### Q5 — Dual Meaning: Think (Dynamic)

Which is correct?

a) "I **think** about the new API architecture right now."  
b) "I **am thinking** about the new API architecture right now." ✅

**Correct:** b  
**Category:** Thinking (think = focus/consider = DYNAMIC here)  
**Reason:** "Think about" = mental activity happening NOW → Continuous form. Compare: "I think (believe) it's good" (stative) vs "I'm thinking about it" (dynamic = considering).

---

### Q6 — Perception: See

Which is correct?

a) "I **see** the bug in line 47 of the code."  
b) "I **am seeing** the bug in line 47 of the code." ✗

**Correct:** a  
**Category:** Perception (see)  
**Reason:** "See" = perceive/understand (stative). ❌ am seeing / ✅ see

---

### Q7 — Perception: Seem/Appear

Which is correct?

a) "The dashboard **seems** to have a memory leak."  
b) "The dashboard **is seeming** to have a memory leak." ✗

**Correct:** a  
**Category:** Perception (seem = appear)  
**Reason:** "Seem/appear" = states of being perceived. Always stative. ❌ is seeming / ✅ seems

---

### Q8 — Possession: Have (Stative)

Which sentence is correct?

a) "We **are having** a server maintenance window scheduled for 8 PM."  
b) "We **have** a server maintenance window scheduled for 8 PM." ✅

**Correct:** b  
**Category:** Possession (have)  
**Reason:** "Have" = own/possess (stative). ❌ are having / ✅ have  
*(Note: "we are having a meeting" is sometimes acceptable = "experiencing," but "have scheduled" = possession → simple)*

# PART 3 — SVA Trap (6 questions)

**Instructions:**
1. Find the main **subject** (noun phrase before the verb)
2. Count: **Singular or Plural?**

---

### Q1 — The number of...

**Subject:** "The number of developers..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"The number of developers _______ increased by 50% this quarter."

a) have  
b) **is** ✓  
c) are  
d) were

**Answer:** b (is)  
**Subject:** "The number" (✅ singular concept)  
**SVA Trap:** "The number of" always takes **singular verb**, even though "developers" is plural.  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #1 — "The number" = singular

---

### Q2 — A number of...

**Subject:** "A number of applications..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"A number of applications _______ already received by HR, and they _______ being reviewed."

a) has / is  
b) **have / are** ✓  
c) has / are  
d) have / is

**Answer:** b (have / are)  
**Subject:** "A number" = plural concept. "Applications" is the real plural focus.  
**SVA Trap:** "A number of" = **plural verb**  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #2 — "A number of" = plural

---

### Q3 — Either...or (with mixed subjects)

**Subject:** "Either the frontend team or the backend developers..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"Either the frontend team or the backend developers _______ required to submit the code review **by Friday**."

a) is  
b) **are** ✓  
c) am  
d) was

**Answer:** b (are)  
**Subject:** Verb agrees with the **NEAREST** noun: "developers" (plural)  
**SVA Trap:** Either...or → agree with closest noun  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #3 — "Either...or" → nearest noun

---

### Q4 — Neither...nor (with mixed subjects)

**Subject:** "Neither the project manager nor the QA engineers..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"Neither the project manager nor the QA engineers _______ informed about the security vulnerability **until this morning**."

a) was ✗  
b) **were** ✓  
c) is  
d) has been

**Answer:** b (were)  
**Subject:** Verb agrees with **NEAREST**: "engineers" (plural)  
**SVA Trap:** Neither...nor → nearest verb agreement  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #4 — "Neither...nor" → nearest noun

---

### Q5 — One of the...

**Subject:** "One of the senior engineers..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"One of the senior engineers _______ developed the original microservices architecture for our platform."

a) have  
b) **has** ✓  
c) are  
d) were

**Answer:** b (has)  
**Subject:** "One" is **always singular**, not "engineers"  
**SVA Trap:** "One of the..." → singular verb  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #5 — "One of the" = singular

---

### Q6 — Police (Exception)

**Subject:** "The police..."

**Singular or Plural?** ___

"The police _______ investigating the data breach that occurred **last week**, and they _______ found 3 entry points."

a) is / have  
b) **are / have** ✓  
c) is / has  
d) are / has

**Answer:** b (are / have)  
**Subject:** "Police" is **always plural** in English (like "people," "staff")  
**SVA Trap:** Police exception → always plural verb  
→ Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #6 — "Police" = always plural

### 📋 ANSWER KEY — Paragraph 1

|---------|----------|---------|-----------|---|---|

### Q1 — Reasoning Test (Incorrect)

**Sentence:** "We have been testing the platform for 2 weeks, and we've found 23 bugs."

**Student's reasoning:** "I see '**for 2 weeks**' → that's Perfect Continuous for the whole sentence."

**Is this reasoning CORRECT or WRONG?** 

a) Correct  
b) **Wrong** ✓

**Explanation:**
- ✅ Part 1 correct: "For 2 weeks" + ongoing → Perfect Continuous "have been testing" ✓
- ❌ Part 2 wrong: "Found 23 bugs" = **countable result** (Rule 4) → Perfect Simple "have found"
- **Correct reasoning:** "For 2 weeks" = Rule 1 (Continuous), BUT "23 bugs" = Rule 4 (Simple). Two different rules in same sentence = TWO different tenses!
- → Xem Cheat Sheet: Có thể mix Perfect Continuous + Perfect Simple trong cùng câu nếu signal words khác nhau

### Q3 — Reasoning Test (Incorrect)

- → Xem Cheat Sheet: SVA Trap #1 — Focus on the MAIN SUBJECT, not the noun after "of"
