# 2 bẫy ngữ pháp hay gặp nhất — Stative Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement

---

## 🎭 **PHẦN 1: STATIVE VERBS** (Không dùng Continuous)

### 🔴 **Cảm xúc — Emotions**
- **like** | **love** | **hate** | **prefer** | **fear**
- ❌ "I am liking" → ✅ "I like"

---

### 🟡 **Tư duy — Thinking & Believing**
- **think*** | **know** | **believe** | **understand** | **remember**
- ❌ "I am thinking it's wrong" → ✅ "I think it's wrong"
- ⚠️ ***think* = dual: "I think so" (opinion) vs "I am thinking about it" (considering)**

---

### 🟢 **Sở hữu — Possession**
- **have*** | **own** | **belong** | **possess**
- ❌ "I am having a pen" → ✅ "I have a pen"
- ⚠️ ***have* = dual: "I have a pen" (own) vs "I am having dinner" (activity)**

---

### 🔵 **Cảm giác — Perception**
- **see*** | **hear** | **smell** | **taste*** | **feel**
- ❌ "I am seeing you" → ✅ "I see you"
- ⚠️ ***see* = dual: "I see you" (perceive) vs "I am seeing my friend" (meet)**
- ⚠️ ***taste* = dual: "It tastes good" (sense) vs "I am tasting the wine" (testing)**

---

### 🟣 **Khác — Other Stative Verbs**
- **seem** | **appear** | **need** | **want** | **cost** | **weigh**
- ❌ "It is seeming difficult" → ✅ "It seems difficult"

---

### ⚡ **QUY TẮC VÀNG — STATIVE VERBS & DUAL MEANINGS**

| **Dual Meaning** | **SAI ❌** | **ĐÚNG ✅** |
|---|---|---|
| **think** (opinion) | "I am thinking he's wrong" | "I think he's wrong" |
| **think** (considering) | — | "I am thinking about your idea" |
| **have** (possess) | "I am having a car" | "I have a car" |
| **have** (activity/event) | — | "I am having a meeting" |
| **see** (perceive) | "I am seeing your point" | "I see your point" |
| **see** (meet) | — | "I am seeing my friend" |

## 📊 **PHẦN 2: SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT TRAPS**

| **QUY TẮC** | **SAI ❌** | **ĐÚNG ✅** |
|---|---|---|
| **The NUMBER of...** (singular) | "The number of students **are** increasing" | "The number of students **is** increasing" |
| **A NUMBER of...** (plural) | "A number of students **is** coming" | "A number of students **are** coming" |
| **Either A or B** (theo B) | "Either the team or players **is** ready" | "Either the team or players **are** ready" |
| **Neither A nor B** (theo B) | "Neither she nor I **am** wrong" | "Neither she nor I **are** wrong" |
| **One of the...** (singular) | "One of the students **are** here" | "One of the students **is** here" |
| **Police** (ngoại lệ) | "The police **is** investigating" | "The police **are** investigating" |

---

### 🎯 **MEMORY TIP**
- **THE number = singular** (cái số lượng như 1 concept)
- **A number = plural** (nhiều cái)
- **Either/Neither → verb theo từ GẦN nhất** (sau "or" hoặc "nor")
- **One of → LUÔN singular** (một cái trong số nhiều)
- **Police → ngoại lệ** (luôn plural ngay cả ở hình thức singular)
