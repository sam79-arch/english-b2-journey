# Which Tense? — Signal Words Master Table

| **TENSE** | **SIGNAL WORDS** | **VÍ DỤ CÂU** | **❌ KHÔNG DÙNG VỚI** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Present Simple** | always, usually, every day, in general | "I drink coffee every morning." | right now, at this moment, yesterday |
| 🟢 **Present Continuous** | now, right now, at the moment, these days | "I am drinking coffee now." | yesterday, last week, every day |
| 🟢 **Present Perfect Simple** | just, already, yet, ever, recently, never, so far, this week/month, it's the first time | "Technology has just transformed the way we communicate." | yesterday, at 5 PM, last week |
| 🟢 **Present Perfect Continuous** | for (3 hours), since, how long, all day | "I have been working for 3 hours." | countable numbers (5 reports, 3 times) |
| 🟠 **Past Simple** | yesterday, last week, ago, at 5 PM, in 2020 | "I finished at 5 PM yesterday." | for (duration completed), right now, yet |
| 🟠 **Past Continuous** | while, when, as, during, at 3 PM yesterday | "I was working when you called." | stative verbs (know, like), standalone |
| 🟣 **Past Perfect Simple** | before, by the time, when (event 1) | "The company had already closed before the policy was implemented." | single past event only, "while" |
| 🟣 **Past Perfect Continuous** | for (duration), how long, before + past | "I had been working for 2 hours when he came." | stative verbs, standalone sentence |

## 🔷 FOR vs SINCE vs BY THE TIME (Special Rules)

| **Từ** | **Khi dùng** | **VÍ DỤ** | **❌ KHÔNG DÙNG VỚI** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FOR** | Khoảng thời gian (duration: 3 hours, 2 weeks) | "I have worked **for** 3 hours." | thời gian cụ thể (yesterday, at 5 PM) |
| **SINCE** | Điểm bắt đầu cụ thể (point: 8 AM, 2020, Monday) | "I have worked **since** 8 AM." | khoảng thời gian (3 hours, 2 weeks) |
| **BY THE TIME** | Trước khi sự kiện khác xảy ra (before event 2) | "**By the time** we arrived, they had left." | standalone (phải có "when" hoặc "if") |

---

### **NGUYÊN TẮC HIGHLIGHT:**
🟢 = PRESENT | 🟠 = PAST | 🟣 = PERFECT | 🔷 = SPECIAL RULES

### **4 QUY TẮC VÀNG (DÙNG NHANH):**
1. **For + duration (vẫn đang làm)?** → Perfect Continuous  
2. **Specific time (yesterday, at 5 PM)?** → Past Simple
3. **Hai sự kiện quá khứ, một trước cái khác?** → Past Perfect
4. **Đếm được số lượng cụ thể (3 reports, twice, the best)?** → Perfect Simple (không dùng Continuous)
