# 🟢 BLOCK 1: PRESENT PERFECT TENSES (Units 11–16)

> From Past → To Now (All have **have/has** + something)

## 📊 OVERVIEW TABLE

| Form | Structure | When | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Present Perfect Simple** | have/has + V3 | Result/Experience (focus: done?) | I **have finished** the report |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | have/has + been + -ing | Duration (focus: how long?) | I **have been working** for 3 hours |

---

## 🎯 UNIT 11: Have / Has / Had (Foundation)

**Form:**
- I/You/We/They: **have**
- He/She/It: **has**
- (Past: had — for all subjects)

**Example:**
```
Present Perfect (Now):
I have visited Paris. (experience)

Past Simple (Complete):
I visited Paris in 2020. (finished, no connection to now)

Past Perfect (Before another action):
I had visited Paris before I met her. (first action: visited, second action: met)
```

---

## 🎯 UNIT 12: Present Perfect Simple (Experiences & Results)

### **Use for:**

1. **Experience (ever/never/how many times?)**
   - "Have you ever tried sushi?" ✓
   - Focus: **Have you had this experience?**

2. **Recent actions (just/recently/so far)**
   - "I have just arrived" (5 minutes ago)
   - "This year we have won 3 games so far"

3. **Results that matter NOW**
   - "I have lost my keys" (consequence: can't find them)
   - "The train has been delayed" (consequence: we'll be late)

### **Signal Words:**
- **Result:** just, already, yet, recently
- **Experience:** ever, never, how many times
- **Range:** this week/month/year, so far

### **Form:**
```
S + have/has + V3
I have finished
You have finished
He/She/It has finished
We/They have finished
```

### **⚠️ Common Trap:**
```
❌ "I am finished" (wrong — not perfect tense)
✓ "I have finished" (perfect: action in past, result now)

❌ "This year I went to Paris" (wrong with time expressions)
✓ "This year I have been to Paris" (perfect for unfinished time period)
```

---

## 🎯 UNIT 13: Present Perfect Continuous (Duration & Process)

### **Use for:**

1. **How long have you been doing X?**
   - "I have been working here for 5 years"
   - Focus: **Duration, the process is important**

2. **Repeated actions recently**
   - "I have been calling you all day" (many calls, still ongoing)

3. **Activity that started in past, continues now**
   - "I have been studying English since 2020" (started 2020, still learning)

### **Form:**
```
S + have/has + been + -ing
I have been working
You have been working
He/She/It has been working
We/They have been working
```

### **Perfect Simple vs Continuous:**

| Perfect Simple | Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|
| "I **have written** 5 reports" | "I **have been writing** for 3 hours" |
| Focus: **Finished count** | Focus: **How long** |
| Answer: How many? ✓ | Answer: How long? ✓ |
| "Have you finished?" | "How long have you been working?" |

---

## 🎯 UNIT 14: Perfect + FOR / SINCE / BY THE TIME

### **FOR vs SINCE:**

| FOR | SINCE |
|---|---|
| **Duration (How long?)** | **Point in time (When?)** |
| "I worked **for** 3 hours" | "I worked **since** 8 AM" |
| "**for** 2 weeks, **for** a year" | "**since** Monday, **since** 2020" |

**Both use Present Perfect Continuous:**
```
I have BEEN working for 3 hours
I have BEEN working since 8 AM
```

### **BY THE TIME:**
```
"By the time" = before (something else happens/happened)
Structure: "By the time + [event], [result]"

Example: "By the time you arrive, I will have finished dinner"
(Two events: you arrive, I finish — I finish FIRST)
```

---

## 🎯 UNIT 15–16: Perfect Simple (Just, Already, Yet) & Experiences

### **JUST / ALREADY / YET:**

| Word | Meaning | Example | Tense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Just** | Very recently | "I have **just** arrived" | Perfect Simple |
| **Already** | Before expected | "I have **already** eaten" | Perfect Simple |
| **Yet** | Still not done (?) / Negative | "Have you finished **yet**?" / "I haven't finished **yet**" | Perfect Simple |

### **Ever / Never:**
```
"Have you EVER been to Tokyo?" — Past experience question
"I have NEVER tried that" — Never had the experience

→ Perfect Simple (experience matters, not time)
```

# 🟠 BLOCK 2: PAST PERFECT TENSES (Units 17–18)

> Before Another Past Action (All have **had** + something)

## 📊 OVERVIEW TABLE

| Form | Structure | When | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Past Perfect Simple** | had + V3 | First of two past actions | **Had finished** → Then arrived |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | had + been + -ing | How long BEFORE past event | **Had been working** when boss arrived |

---

## 🎯 UNIT 17: Past Perfect Simple (Had...)

### **Use for:**
**Two events in past, need to show WHICH ONE HAPPENED FIRST**

```
Timeline:
1. He finished work (FIRST) ← Past Perfect
2. He went home (SECOND) ← Past Simple

Sentence: "He HAD FINISHED work before he WENT home"
          (Had + V3)          (Past simple)
```

### **Form:**
```
S + had + V3
He had finished
She had left
They had arrived
```

### **Common Structure:**
```
"By the time" + [past simple event], [past perfect event]

Example: "By the time I understood, she HAD ALREADY LEFT"
```

### **⚠️ Common Trap:**
```
❌ "He finished before he went home" (both sound equal)
✓ "He HAD finished before he went home" (clear order: finish → go)

❌ "When the teacher entered, students had talked" (weird)
✓ "When the teacher entered, students WERE talking" (Past Continuous)
✓ "When the teacher entered, students HAD ALREADY TALKED" (P. Perfect: activity finished before teacher came)
```

---

## 🎯 UNIT 18: Past Perfect Continuous (Had Been -ing)

### **Use for:**
**HOW LONG had someone been doing X when Y happened?**

```
Timeline:
- Started: He came home
- For 3 hours: He had been working
- Then: His wife called

Sentence: "He HAD BEEN WORKING FOR 3 HOURS when his wife called"
```

### **Form:**
```
S + had + been + -ing
I had been working
You had been working
He/She/It had been working
We/They had been working
```

### **Signal: FOR + time duration**
```
"I HAD BEEN WAITING FOR 30 MINUTES before the bus arrived"
"She HAD BEEN STUDYING FOR 2 HOURS when the power cut"
```

# 🔵 BLOCK 3: FUTURE TENSES (Units 19–20)

> Talking About Tomorrow/Next Year (Multiple ways!)

## 📊 OVERVIEW TABLE

| Form | Structure | When | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Will** | will + base | Decision now / Prediction | I **will** call you |
| **Going to** | am/is/are going to + base | Plan decided / Evidence | I'm **going to** leave |
| **Present Continuous** | am/is/are + -ing | Scheduled plan | I **am leaving** tomorrow at 5 PM |
| **Present Simple** | present simple | Timetable / Schedule | The train **leaves** at 8 AM |

---

## 🎯 UNIT 19: Will + Base Verb

### **Use for:**

1. **Decision at the moment of speaking**
   - "I will help you" (decide now)

2. **Prediction / Expectation**
   - "It will rain tomorrow" (expectation)

3. **Promise / Offer**
   - "I will call you back" (promise)
   - "If you need help, I will assist" (offer)

### **Form:**
```
S + will + base verb
I will go
You will go
He/She/It will go
We/They will go

Negative: won't (will not)
Question: Will you go?
```

### **⚠️ Common Trap:**
```
❌ "If you will come, I will help" (never use will after if)
✓ "If you come, I will help" (will only in result clause)
```

---

## 🎯 UNIT 20: Going To + Base Verb

### **Use for:**

1. **Plan / Intention decided before now**
   - "I am going to study tomorrow" (already planned)

2. **Evidence / Obvious future**
   - "Look at the sky! It's going to rain" (clouds visible)

### **Form:**
```
S + am/is/are + going to + base verb
I am going to leave
He/She/It is going to leave
We/They are going to leave
```

### **Will vs Going To:**

| WILL | GOING TO |
|---|---|
| Decision **NOW** | Decision **BEFORE NOW** |
| "I will resign!" (just decided) | "I am going to resign" (planned earlier) |
| Prediction (no evidence) | Obvious future (evidence) |
| "I think it will rain" | "It's going to rain!" (dark clouds) |

---

## 🎯 PRESENT CONTINUOUS & PRESENT SIMPLE for FUTURE

### **Present Continuous for Future:**
```
"Are you coming tomorrow?" (scheduled activity)
Usually near future, fixed plan
```

### **Present Simple for Future:**
```
"The meeting starts at 3 PM" (official timetable)
"When does your flight leave?" (schedule/timetable)
```

# 🟣 BONUS: CONDITIONALS (Optional — For Reference)

> "If X happens, Y will happen"

## 📊 THREE TYPES

| Type | If-Clause | Result | When | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cond 1** | Present Simple | will + V | Real, likely future | If you study, you **will** pass |
| **Cond 2** | Past Simple (were) | would + V | Hypothetical, unlikely | If you studied, you **would** pass |
| **Cond 3** | Had + V3 | would have + V3 | Past, too late | If you had studied, you **would have** passed |

### **Key Rule: NEVER use "will" in If-clause!**
```
❌ "If you will study, you will pass"
✓ "If you study, you will pass" (Conditional 1)
```

# 🎯 QUICK DECISION TREE

**Choose the right tense:**

```
Is the action FINISHED?
├─ YES: Does it have a RESULT NOW?
│   ├─ YES → Present Perfect Simple
│   └─ NO → Past Simple
│
└─ NO: Is it CONTINUING NOW?
    ├─ YES → Present Continuous / Present Perfect Continuous
    └─ NO → Future (will / going to / present simple)
```

| Mistake | Wrong ❌ | Right ✅ | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Using "will" in if-clause | If you will come, I will help | If you come, I will help | 19-20 |
| Mixing perfect tenses | I am finished | I have finished | 12 |
| Wrong signal with perfect | I finished last week | I have finished last week | 12 |
| FOR/SINCE confusion | I work for 3 years here | I have been working here for 3 years | 14 |
| Past perfect clarity | When he arrived, he left | When he arrived, he had left | 17 |
| Going to vs Will | I go to buy bread | I'm going to buy bread OR I will buy bread | 20 |
| Two past events | When I finished, I called | When I finished, I had called | 17 |
