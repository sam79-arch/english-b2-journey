# **UNIT 1: PRESENT SIMPLE vs PRESENT CONTINUOUS**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Hành động được miêu tả — nó đang xảy ra NGAY BÂY GIỜ?
├─ YES → Present Continuous (I am working)
└─ NO → Tiếp tục...
    │
    ├─ Nó là thói quen / lẽ thường / sự thật chung?
    │  YES → Present Simple (I work)
    │
    ├─ Nó là khả năng / tính chất?
    │  YES → Present Simple (She is clever)
    │
    └─ Nó là lịch trình / kế hoạch chính thức?
       YES → Present Simple (The train leaves at 5 PM)
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Aspect | Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| **Khi dùng** | Thói quen, sự thật, khả năng, tính chất | Hành động đang xảy ra / vừa xảy ra |
| **Signal words** | always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, in general | now, right now, at the moment, these days, currently |
| **Example** | *I drink coffee every morning.* | *I am drinking coffee now.* |
| **Lỗi thường** | ❌ "I am liking coffee" (sai — like là stative) | ✓ "I like coffee" (thích là cảm xúc lâu dài) |
| **Cấu trúc** | S + V (infinitive) | S + am/is/are + V-ing |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 2 Example:
```
"Many people work in offices every day, and they are spending 
most of their time at their computers."

→ Câu hợp lệ: 
- "Many people work in offices every day" ✓ (thói quen)
- "they spend most of their time at their computers" ✓ (thực trạng chung)

[IELTS] Chú ý: Tasks 2 dùng nhiều Present Simple hơn Continuous 
vì bạn nói về xu hướng/tình huống chung, không hành động cụ thể "ngay bây giờ"
```

### Listening Example:
```
Speaker A: "What are you doing right now?"
Speaker B: "I am studying English grammar."

→ Continuous được dùng vì hành động xảy ra tại thời điểm nói.
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: Stative verbs với Continuous**

❌ "She is understanding the concept." (understand = tư duy, không dùng Continuous)  
✓ "She understands the concept."  
**Lý do:** "Understand" là stative → dùng Simple

---

**Trap 2: Habit vs Right Now**

❌ "I am going to the gym three times a week." (sai vì ba lần mỗi tuần là habit)  
✓ "I go to the gym three times a week."  
**Lý do:** "Three times a week" = thói quen → dùng Simple

---

**Trap 3: Chính thức lịch trình**

❌ "The conference is starting at 3 PM tomorrow." (sai vì là lịch trình chính thức)  
✓ "The conference starts at 3 PM tomorrow."  
**Lý do:** Timetables, schedules → dùng Simple, không Continuous

---

## 📝 Mini Practice (5 questions)

**1.** "The sun _______ in the east." (basic)
- a) rises
- b) is rising
- c) both are correct

**Answer:** a) rises | **Lý do:** Thực tế khoa học vũ trụ → Simple

---

**2.** "My brother _______ at a tech company right now, and he _______ software development." (mid)
- a) works / develops
- b) is working / is developing
- c) works / is developing

**Answer:** b) is working / is developing | **Lý do:** "Right now" → Continuous; nhưng "right now" ám chỉ anh đang làm cái gì ở thời điểm nói → develop phải là Continuous

---

**3.** "She _______ three languages fluently." (mid)
- a) speaks
- b) is speaking
- c) both correct

**Answer:** a) speaks | **Lý do:** Skill / khả năng → Simple, không Continuous

---

**4.** "The government _______ new policies to address climate change, and these initiatives _______ environmental protection significantly." [IELTS - Task 2] (high)
- a) is implementing / promote
- b) implements / are promoting
- c) is implementing / are promoting

**Answer:** b) implements / are promoting | **Lý do:** Writing Task 2 dùng Simple cho xu hướng chung ("initiatives ... protect" = tác động thường xuyên)

---

**5.** Error correction: "He is believing that success requires hard work." (high)
- Sửa lại: "He believes that success requires hard work."
- **Lý do:** "Believe" là stative (tư duy, ý kiến) → không dùng Continuous

# **UNIT 2: STATIVE VERBS — The Silent Killer**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Động từ này miêu tả
├─ Cảm xúc / Tư duy / Sở hữu / Cảm giác?
│  YES → Có thể là STATIVE → Không dùng Continuous
│
└─ Hành động, chuyển động, thay đổi trạng thái?
   YES → DYNAMIC → Có thể dùng Continuous
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Stative Verbs | Bạn hay sai ở đây vì... | ❌ Sai | ✓ Đúng |
|---|---|---|---|
| **like, love, hate** | Cảm xúc = không dùng -ing | "I am liking this coffee" | "I like this coffee" |
| **think, believe, know** | Ý kiến = không dùng -ing | "She is thinking he's wrong" | "She thinks he's wrong" |
| **have (sở hữu)** | Sở hữu = không dùng -ing | "I am having a car" | "I have a car" |
| **see, hear, smell** | Cảm giác tự nhiên = không -ing | "I am seeing you" | "I see you" |
| **seem, appear** | Vẻ ngoài = không dùng -ing | "He is seeming happy" | "He seems happy" |
| **belong, possess** | Sở hữu = không dùng -ing | "This is belonging to me" | "This belongs to me" |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 1 Example:
```
"The graph shows that the number of users is increasing every year."

NOT: "The graph is showing..."
Lý do: "show" trong context "graph shows data" = stative (thực tế)

[IELTS] Writing Task 1 dùng nhiều stative verbs:
- The data shows / The chart indicates / The table reveals
- Housing prices seem to be rising
```

### Listening Example:
```
Examiner: "What do you think about your studies?"
Student: "I think it's interesting. I enjoy studying English."

→ Think, enjoy = stative → không bao giờ dùng Continuous trong tư duy.
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: Think (opinion) vs Think (consider)**

❌ "I am thinking you are wrong." (lầm tưởng think như action)  
✓ "I think you are wrong." (ý kiến)  
**Nhưng:** ✓ "I am thinking about your proposal." (hành động = đang suy nghĩ)  
**Lý do:** "think" có 2 nghĩa: stative (opinion) vs dynamic (consider)

---

**Trap 2: Have (sở hữu) vs Have (hoạt động)**

❌ "I am having a pencil." (sai — sở hữu)  
✓ "I have a pencil."  
**Nhưng:** ✓ "I am having a meeting." (hành động = đang họp)  
**Lý do:** "have" có 2 nghĩa: stative (own) vs dynamic (experience)

---

**Trap 3: See (cảm giác) vs See (gặp)**

❌ "I am seeing your point." (sai — nhìn bằng mắt tâm)  
✓ "I see your point."  
**Nhưng:** ✓ "I am seeing my friend later." (hành động = sắp gặp)  
**Lý do:** "see" có 2 nghĩa: stative (perceive) vs dynamic (meet)

---

## 📝 Mini Practice (5 questions)

**1.** "I _______ this jacket, so I am giving it to you." (basic)
- a) am loving
- b) love
- c) am loving — love (both needed)

**Answer:** b) love | **Lý do:** "Love" = cảm xúc → stative → Simple

---

**2.** "At the moment, she _______ about her exam results, and she _______ very worried." (mid)
- a) thinks / is
- b) is thinking / is
- c) thinks / looks

**Answer:** b) is thinking / is | **Lý do:** "At the moment" → think ở đây = hành động suy nghĩ (dynamic); "is" = state (stative)

---

**3.** "He _______ a terrible headache right now, so he _______ the meeting early." (mid)
- a) has / is leaving
- b) is having / is leaving
- c) has / leaves

**Answer:** b) is having / is leaving | **Lý do:** "Right now" + "headache" = trạng thái hiện tại → Continuous

---

**4.** "The research _______ that companies _______ their workforce during economic downturns." [IELTS - Reading] (high)
- a) is showing / are reducing
- b) shows / reduce
- c) is showing / reduce

**Answer:** b) shows / reduce | **Lý do:** Research = stative; general truth = Simple

---

**5.** Error correction: "I am knowing your sister since 2020." (high)
- Sửa: "I have known your sister since 2020."
- **Lý do:** "Know" = stative → không dùng Continuous; "since 2020" = Present Perfect (không Present Continuous)

# **UNIT 3: PRESENT PERFECT SIMPLE vs CONTINUOUS**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Hành động bắt đầu từ quá khứ đến bây giờ:
├─ Focus vào QUỸ THỜI GIAN / QUỸ TRÌNH DIỄN RA?
│  YES → Present Perfect CONTINUOUS (I have been working for 3 hours)
│
├─ Focus vào KẾT QUẢ / SỐ LƯỢNG?
│  YES → Present Perfect SIMPLE (I have completed 5 projects)
│
├─ Signal word: already, yet, just?
│  YES → SIMPLE (I have already finished)
│
└─ Signal word: for X hours, all day, how long?
   YES → CONTINUOUS (I have been working all day)
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Aspect | Present Perfect Simple | Present Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Kết quả / Số lượng / Hoàn thành | Quỹ thời gian / Quá trình / Tiếp diễn |
| **Signal words** | already, yet, just, ever, times, number | for, since, how long, all week, recently |
| **Example** | *I have written 3 reports.* | *I have been writing reports for 2 hours.* |
| **Bạn hay sai** | ❌ "I have been writing 3 reports" (sai — không đếm được) | ✓ "I have written 3 reports" (số lượng → Simple) |
| **Tại sao sai** | Continuous nhấn quá trình, không countable results | Simple nhấn kết quả có thể đếm được |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 2 Example:
```
"Over the past decade, companies have invested billions in renewable energy, 
and they have created thousands of new jobs in this sector."

→ "have invested" = Simple (kết quả lớn)
→ "have created" = Simple (số lượng cụ thể)

[IELTS] Writing Task 2: Continuous ít dùng hơn Simple vì bạn nói kết quả, 
không process. Chỉ dùng Continuous khi nhấn mạnh "đang diễn ra" (ít phổ biến).
```

### Listening Example:
```
Interviewer: "How long have you been studying English?"
Interviewee: "I have been studying for about 5 years."

→ "How long" + "for 5 years" = Continuous (quỹ thời gian)
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: Countable results vs Duration**

❌ "I have been writing 10 emails." (sai — countable → Simple)  
✓ "I have written 10 emails." (Simple)  
✓ "I have been writing emails for 2 hours." (Continuous — duration, không countable)  
**Lý do:** Số lượng cụ thể (10) → Simple; duration (2 hours) → Continuous

---

**Trap 2: Already + Continuous**

❌ "I have already been finishing." (sai — already → Simple)  
✓ "I have already finished."  
**Lý do:** "Already" = kết quả hoàn thành → Simple

---

**Trap 3: Since + Continuous (nhưng vẫn đang làm)**

❌ "I have been working on this since morning, and I haven't finished." → Đúng! (quỹ thời gian dài)  
✓ "I have been working on this since morning, and I haven't finished." (Continuous)  
**Nhưng:** ✓ "I have worked 5 hours since morning." (Simple — countable)  
**Lý do:** "Since morning" = quỹ thời gian, vẫn đang làm → Continuous

---

## 📝 Mini Practice (5 questions)

**1.** "How many reports _______ you _______?" (basic)
- a) have / been writing
- b) have / written
- c) have been / writing

**Answer:** b) have / written | **Lý do:** "How many" = countable → Simple

---

**2.** "She _______ on this project since 8 AM, and she _______ tired." (mid)
- a) has been working / is
- b) has worked / is
- c) is working / feels

**Answer:** a) has been working / is | **Lý do:** "Since 8 AM" (quỹ thời gian) → Continuous; "is tired" = state hiện tại

---

**3.** "The team _______ three different strategies, and they _______ testing the most promising one." (mid)
- a) have developed / are
- b) have been developing / have been
- c) developed / were

**Answer:** a) have developed / are | **Lý do:** "3 strategies" = countable → Simple; "testing" = đang làm → Continuous

---

**4.** "Since the new policy was introduced, productivity _______ by 25%, and employees _______ more satisfied with their work conditions." [IELTS - Task 2] (high)
- a) has increased / have been feeling
- b) has been increasing / are feeling
- c) increased / felt

**Answer:** a) has increased / have been feeling | **Lý do:** "25%" = countable result → Simple; "feeling" = trạng thái hiện tại từ quá khứ → có thể dùng cả Simple "are feeling" or Continuous; trong IELTS, usually Simple cho kết quả cụ thể

---

**5.** Error correction: "I have been studying three languages for years now." (high)
- Sửa: "I have studied three languages for years now."
- **Lý do:** "Three languages" = countable results → Simple, không Continuous

# **UNIT 4: PAST SIMPLE vs PAST CONTINUOUS**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Sự kiện xảy ra trong quá khứ:
├─ Nó hoàn thành? Nó có điểm bắt đầu & kết thúc rõ ràng?
│  YES → Past SIMPLE (I finished at 5 PM)
│
└─ Nó đang xảy ra khi có sự kiện khác?
   YES → Past CONTINUOUS (I was working when he called)
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Aspect | Past Simple | Past Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| **Khi dùng** | Sự kiện hoàn thành (điểm bắt đầu & kết thúc) | Sự kiện đang xảy ra trong quá khứ |
| **Signal words** | yesterday, last week, ago, at 5 PM, in 2020 | while, when, as, during, at 3 PM yesterday |
| **Example** | *I worked for 8 hours yesterday.* | *I was working when you called.* |
| **Cấu trúc** | S + V-ed | S + was/were + V-ing |
| **IELTS Pattern** | Hay dùng trong Task 1 (thống kê quá khứ), Task 2 (ví dụ) | Ít dùng hơn, chủ yếu trong story/narrative |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 1 Example:
```
"In 2015, the company produced 50% more goods than in 2010. 
The expansion occurred due to increased demand."

→ Past Simple (hoàn thành, điểm cụ thể trong quá khứ)

[IELTS] Writing Task 1: Luôn dùng Past Simple khi mô tả thay đổi, 
con số cụ thể trong thời gian quá khứ.
```

### Speaking Example:
```
Examiner: "What were you doing when I called?"
Student: "I was studying English grammar."

→ "Was studying" = Continuous (hành động đang xảy ra)
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: Khi dùng "when"**

❌ "When I studied, my sister was sleeping." (sai — when + simple thường là liên tiếp)  
✓ "When I was studying, my sister was sleeping." (cùng lúc)  
**Hoặc:** ✓ "When I finished studying, my sister woke up." (liên tiếp)  
**Lý do:** "When" + Simple = hành động liên tiếp; "When" + Continuous = cùng lúc

---

**Trap 2: Duration + completed**

❌ "I was working for 3 hours yesterday." (sai — for + thời gian hoàn thành)  
✓ "I worked for 3 hours yesterday." (Simple — hoàn thành rồi)  
✓ "I was working for 3 hours when he called." (Continuous — đang làm khi có sự kiện)  
**Lý do:** "For 3 hours" + quá khứ hoàn thành → Simple; nhưng "while X" hoặc "when Y happened" → Continuous

---

**Trap 3: Stative verbs không dùng Continuous**

❌ "I was knowing the answer when the exam started." (sai — know = stative)  
✓ "I knew the answer when the exam started."  
**Lý do:** Stative verbs không dùng Continuous ngay cả trong quá khứ

---

## 📝 Mini Practice (5 questions)

**1.** "Yesterday, I _______ from 9 AM to 5 PM." (basic)
- a) worked
- b) was working
- c) have been working

**Answer:** a) worked | **Lý do:** Hành động hoàn thành, cho biết giờ cụ thể → Simple

---

**2.** "While I _______ on my assignment, the power _______ out." (mid)
- a) worked / went
- b) was working / went
- c) worked / was going

**Answer:** b) was working / went | **Lý do:** "Was working" = Continuous (đang xảy ra); "went out" = Simple (sự kiện đột ngột)

---

**3.** "She _______ the door and _______ me reading a book." (mid)
- a) opened / saw
- b) was opening / was seeing
- c) opened / was seeing

**Answer:** c) opened / was seeing | **Lý do:** Hành động liên tiếp → Simple; "saw me + V-ing" → saw là Simple, nhưng miêu tả tình cảnh đang xảy ra (was reading)

---

**4.** "At 3 PM yesterday, journalists _______ questions about the government's plan when a surprising announcement _______." [IELTS - Listening] (high)
- a) asked / made
- b) were asking / was made
- c) were asking / was being made

**Answer:** b) were asking / was made | **Lý do:** "At 3 PM yesterday" = thời điểm cụ thể → Continuous; announcement = bị động, đột ngột

---

**5.** Error correction: "I was living in London for 5 years." (high)
- Sửa: "I lived in London for 5 years."
- **Lý do:** Khoảng thời gian hoàn thành trong quá khứ → Simple, không Continuous

# **UNIT 5: PAST PERFECT SIMPLE vs CONTINUOUS**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Hai sự kiện trong quá khứ, một xảy ra trước cái kia:
├─ Focus vào SỰ KIỆN HOÀN THÀNH (hành động thứ nhất)?
│  YES → Past Perfect SIMPLE (I had finished before he arrived)
│
└─ Focus vào QUỸ TRÌNH / KHOẢNG THỜI GIAN?
   YES → Past Perfect CONTINUOUS (I had been working for 2 hours when he arrived)
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Aspect | Past Perfect Simple | Past Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| **Khi dùng** | Khi muốn nhấn một hành động đã xong trước hành động khác | Nhấn khoảng thời gian hoặc quá trình của hành động trước |
| **Signal** | by the time, before, when, after (hành động 1 hoàn thành) | after ... for, by the time + duration, for how long |
| **Example** | *I had finished dinner before he arrived.* | *I had been working for 2 hours when he arrived.* |
| **Bạn hay sai** | ❌ Quên dùng Past Perfect khi có deux hành động quá khứ | ❌ Dùng Continuous khi chỉ muốn nói hành động hoàn thành |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 2 Example:
```
"By the time the government had implemented the new policy, 
hundreds of businesses had already closed."

→ Past Perfect (2 hành động quá khứ, one trước cái kia)

[IELTS] Ít dùng Past Perfect trong Writing Task 2 vì bạn thường 
nói about current issues, not historical sequence.
```

### Reading Example:
```
"The company had been struggling financially for decades 
before they finally declared bankruptcy."

→ Previous struggle (continuous) BEFORE bankruptcy
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: 2 hành động quá khứ — khi nào dùng Perfect?**

❌ "I finished my work and left the office." (2 hành động liên tiếp → Simple OK)  
✓ "I had finished my work before I left the office." (muốn nhấn thứ tự → Perfect)  
**Lý do:** Simple = liên tiếp; Perfect = nhấn một xảy ra trước

---

**Trap 2: Perfect Continuous với khoảng thời gian dài**

❌ "I had finished the project for 2 hours when the boss asked about it." (sai)  
✓ "I had been working on the project for 2 hours when the boss asked about it." (Continuous)  
✓ "I had finished the project before the boss asked about it." (Simple)  
**Lý do:** "For 2 hours" = duration → Continuous

---

**Trap 3: Stative verbs không dùng Perfect Continuous**

❌ "I had been knowing the answer before the exam." (sai)  
✓ "I had known the answer before the exam."  
**Lý do:** "Know" = stative

---

**1.** "By 5 PM, I _______ my report." (basic)
- a) had finished
- b) had been finishing
- c) finished

**Answer:** a) had finished | **Lý do:** Hành động hoàn thành trước thời điểm khác → Past Perfect Simple

---

**2.** "She _______ on the project for two weeks _______ she received the promotion." (mid)
- a) had worked / when
- b) had been working / when
- c) worked / before

**Answer:** b) had been working / when | **Lý do:** "For two weeks" = duration → Perfect Continuous

---

**3.** "They _______ three meetings _______ the decision was announced." (mid)
- a) had held / before
- b) had been holding / when
- c) held / by the time

**Answer:** a) had held / before | **Lý do:** 3 meetings = countable → Simple

---

**4.** "By the time the merger was completed, the company _______ in negotiations _______ over a year." [IELTS - Reading] (high)
- a) was / for
- b) had been / for
- c) had been engaged / since

**Answer:** b/c) had been / for or had been engaged / since | **Lý do:** Duration dài + hành động trước → Perfect Continuous

---

**5.** Error correction: "The scientists had been discovering the cure before they started testing on animals." (high)
- Sửa: "The scientists had discovered the cure before they started testing on animals."
- **Lý do:** Hành động hoàn thành rõ ràng, không cần nhấn quá trình → Simple

# **UNIT 6: SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT — Your Silent Error Maker**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Xác định SUBJECT:
├─ Singular (1)?
│  ├─ "the number of + plural" → SINGULAR (the number of students IS...)
│  └─ Tên riêng, không đếm được → SINGULAR
│
├─ Plural (>1)?
│  ├─ "a number of + plural" → PLURAL (a number of students ARE...)
│  └─ "either A or B / neither A nor B" → Phải xem cái gần nhất
│  └─ Collective nouns → Singular (UK) hoặc Plural (US + emphasis on individuals)
│
└─ Khó xác định? → Xem "Bonus 3: Collective Noun Guide"
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Trường hợp | Rule | ❌ Sai | ✓ Đúng |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"the number of"** | Luôn SINGULAR | "The number of students ARE..." | "The number of students IS..." |
| **"a number of"** | Luôn PLURAL | "A number of students IS..." | "A number of students ARE..." |
| **"either A or B"** | Phù hợp với cái GẦN nhất | "Either the manager or the staff is..." | "Either the manager or the staff are..." |
| **"neither A nor B"** | Phù hợp với cái GẦN nhất | "Neither she nor I am..." | "Neither she nor I are..." |
| **"one of the"** | SINGULAR | "One of the students are..." | "One of the students is..." |
| **Collective nouns** | UK = Singular, US = Plural | "The team are playing..." (US OK) | "The team is playing..." (UK standard) |
| **Uncountable** | SINGULAR | "The data are..." (sai UK) | "The data is..." (UK); "The data are..." (US) |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 2 Example:
```
"The number of people who have smartphones HAS increased dramatically, 
and a number of studies ARGUE that technology is beneficial."

→ "The number" = Singular (HAS)
→ "A number of" = Plural (ARGUE)

[IELTS] This is a VERY common mistake. Examiners mark this as 
a grammar error. Must get it right!
```

### Listening Example:
```
"Neither the government nor the private sector has invested enough."
→ "has" (singular) vì "private sector" (gần nhất) là singular
```

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: The number of vs A number of** (BẠN THƯỜNG SAI)

❌ "The number of students ARE increasing."  
✓ "The number of students IS increasing."  
✓ "A number of students ARE increasing."  
**Lý do:** THE NUMBER OF = singular (số lượng như một concept); A NUMBER OF = plural (nhiều cái)

---

**Trap 2: Either/Neither + gần nhất**

❌ "Either the teacher or the students IS responsible."  
✓ "Either the teacher or the students ARE responsible." (gần nhất = students = plural)  
✓ "Either the students or the teacher IS responsible." (gần nhất = teacher = singular)  
**Lý do:** Phải match với subject GẦN nhất (cái sau "or")

---

**Trap 3: Collective nouns + UK vs US**

❌ (UK) "The team are playing." (không chuẩn UK — phải singular)  
✓ (UK) "The team is playing."  
✓ (US) "The team are playing." (US chấp nhận plural khi nhấn individuals)  
**Lý do:** IELTS = International, theo UK standard → dùng Singular cho collective nouns

# **UNIT 7: PRESENT PERFECT vs PAST SIMPLE**

## 🔄 Decision Flowchart

```
Hành động xảy ra trong quá khứ — bạn muốn nói:
├─ "Nó xảy ra KHI NÀO cụ thể?" (có thời gian rõ ràng)
│  YES → Past SIMPLE (I finished at 5 PM yesterday)
│
├─ "Nó xảy ra bao lâu rồi?" (không có thời gian cụ thể)
│  YES → Present PERFECT (I have finished)
│
└─ "Nó vẫn còn ảnh hưởng đến bây giờ?"
   YES → Present PERFECT (I have fixed the bug, and it works now)
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Aspect | Past Simple | Present Perfect |
|---|---|---|
| **Thời gian** | Thời gian cụ thể (when) | Không xác định hoặc liên quan đến bây giờ |
| **Signal** | yesterday, last week, in 2020, at 5 PM, ago | just, already, yet, ever, recently, for X time |
| **Example** | *I finished at 5 PM yesterday.* | *I have just finished.* |
| **Cảm giác** | Đã qua, không liên quan bây giờ | Mới xảy ra, còn ảnh hưởng hiện tại |
| **IELTS** | Writing Task 1 (data cũ), Task 2 (ví dụ) | Writing Task 2 (affect hiện tại) |

---

## 🎓 IELTS In Context

### Writing Task 1 Example:
```
"In 2015, the company generated £5 million in revenue."
→ Past Simple (time specific: 2015)

NOT: "The company has generated..."
```

### Writing Task 2 Example:
```
"Technology has revolutionized the way we communicate, and it 
has created both opportunities and challenges."

→ Present Perfect (still affecting us now, no specific time)
→ If you said "Technology revolutionized..." = implies it stopped affecting us
```

---

## 🚨 Trap Spotter

**Trap 1: "For" khác nhau trong quá khứ vs hiện tại**

❌ "I have lived in London for 10 years, and I moved there in 2016." (lẫn lộn)  
✓ "I have lived in London for 10 years." (hiện tại → vẫn sống)  
✓ "I lived in London for 10 years and moved to Paris in 2016." (quá khứ → đã dừng)  
**Lý do:** Present Perfect → vẫn đang xảy ra; Past Simple → đã kết thúc

---

**Trap 2: Recently vs Ago**

❌ "I have finished the project ago." (sai — ago dùng với Past Simple)  
✓ "I finished the project 2 days ago."  
✓ "I have recently finished the project."  
**Lý do:** "Ago" = Past Simple; "Recently" = Present Perfect

---

**Trap 3: "Have you ever" vs "Did you ever"**

❌ "Did you ever travel to Japan?" (sai grammar — "ever" → Present Perfect)  
✓ "Have you ever traveled to Japan?" (hỏi trải nghiệm)  
**Nhưng:** ✓ "When did you travel to Japan?" (hỏi thời gian cụ thể)  
**Lý do:** "Ever" = life experience → Present Perfect

# **UNIT 8: FOR vs SINCE vs BY THE TIME**

## 🔄 Timeline Explanation

```
                    PAST ————————————> NOW (Hiện tại)
                    |              |
                  Point  ←—— Duration ——→
                   (since)        (for)

SINCE = điểm bắt đầu (point in time)
FOR = khoảng thời gian (duration)
BY THE TIME = trước khi X xảy ra
```

---

## 📊 Contrast Table

| Từ | Khi dùng | Cấu trúc | ❌ Sai | ✓ Đúng |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FOR** | Khoảng thời gian (2 hours, 5 days) | "FOR + duration" | "I have worked since 3 hours" | "I have worked for 3 hours" |
| **SINCE** | Điểm bắt đầu cụ thể (2020, Monday, 8 AM) | "SINCE + point in time" | "I have worked for 2020" | "I have worked since 2020" |
| **BY THE TIME** | Trước khi sự kiện khác xảy ra | "BY THE TIME + clause" | "By the time we arrived, they finished" | "By the time we arrived, they had finished" |

---

## 📝 5 IELTS Common Sentences

**1.** "I have been studying English **for** three years now."  
→ Duration (3 years) → FOR

**2.** "The company has been operating **since** 1995."  
→ Point in time (1995) → SINCE

**3.** "By the time the government **had announced** the policy, many companies **had already** adapted."  
→ Past Perfect (2 events, one before the other)

**4.** "**For** the past decade, technology has transformed industries across the globe."  
→ Duration (decade) → FOR (Writing Task 2 pattern)

**5.** "**Since** the introduction of AI, productivity has increased substantially."  
→ Point in time (introduction) → SINCE (Writing Task 2 pattern)

# **BONUS SECTION 1: MASTER TENSE CHEAT SHEET**

| Tense | Signal Words | Example | Cannot use with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, in general | The sun rises in the east | "right now", "at this moment" |
| **Present Continuous** | now, right now, at the moment, currently, these days | I am working on a project | "yesterday", "every day" |
| **Present Perfect** | just, already, yet, ever, recently, for X time, since | I have finished my work | specific time (yesterday, last week) |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | for X time, since, how long, all week | I have been working for 3 hours | countable results (5 reports) |
| **Past Simple** | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, at 5 PM | I finished at 5 PM | "for X time" (duration completed) |
| **Past Continuous** | while, when, as, during, at 3 PM yesterday | I was working when you called | static/stative verbs |
| **Past Perfect** | before, by the time, when (previous event) | I had finished before he arrived | without context of 2 events |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | for + duration, how long (before past event) | I had been working for 2 hours when he arrived | stative verbs |
| **Future Simple** | tomorrow, next week, will, shall | I will finish tomorrow | present conditions (unless: if...) |

# **BONUS SECTION 2: STATIVE VERBS MASTER LIST**

## 📋 Organized by Category

### 🎭 **Emotions & Feelings**
- **like, love, hate, prefer** | Can't use Continuous. "I like coffee" ✓ / "I am liking coffee" ❌
- **fear, adore, detest** | Only Simple form accepted

### 🧠 **Thinking & Believing**
- **think** (opinion: "I think it's true") | Simple ✓ / but "think about" (considering) = can use Continuous ✓
- **believe, suppose, consider (opinion)** | Only Simple. "I believe it" ✓
- **know, understand, realize** | Only Simple. "I know it" ✓
- **remember, forget, recall** | Only Simple. "I remember you" ✓

### 👜 **Possession & Relationship**
- **have (own)** | Simple ✓ / but "have (experience)" = can use Continuous ✓. "I have a pen" ✓ / "I am having a meeting" ✓
- **own, possess, belong** | Only Simple

### 👁️ **Perception & Sensation**
- **see (perceive)** | Simple ✓ / but "see (meet)" = can use Continuous ✓. "I see your point" ✓ / "I am seeing my friend" ✓
- **hear, smell, taste, feel (physical)** | Only Simple. "I hear music" ✓
- **look (appear), seem, appear, sound** | Only Simple. "It seems correct" ✓

### 🔄 **Other Stative Verbs**
- **be, exist, cost, weigh, measure** | Only Simple
- **need, want, require** | Usually Simple (but can vary in creative contexts)

**💡 Rule:** Nếu động từ miêu tả **trạng thái, ý kiến, cảm xúc, sở hữu** → **không dùng Continuous**

| **committee** | is | are | Singular | The committee has decided. |

# **BONUS SECTION 4: FOR vs SINCE vs BY THE TIME — Timeline + Examples**

## Timeline Visualization

```
For = Duration (how long?) |———————5 years———————|
                          Start                   End (Now)

Since = Point (when did it start?) 
                              2020 —————————→ Now
                              (point)    (continuing)

By The Time = Sequence (before another action?)
                      Event 1          Event 2
                    (had done)       (when X)
```

---

## 5 IELTS-Style Sentences

**1. FOR + Present Perfect Continuous**
```
"Companies have been investing in renewable energy for the past 
five years, and the results are promising."

→ Duration: 5 years (ongoing)
```

**2. SINCE + Present Perfect Simple or Continuous**
```
"Since the introduction of smartphones, communication has become 
instantaneous, and people have been connecting globally."

→ Point in time: introduction (still relevant)
```

**3. BY THE TIME + Past Perfect**
```
"By the time researchers had published their findings, the 
pharmaceutical company had already invested millions."

→ Sequence: both completed, one before the other
```

**4. FOR + Past Duration**
```
"The company operated in loss for three years before 
finally becoming profitable."

→ Duration in past (3 years, now complete)
```

**5. SINCE + Past Perfect Continuous**
```
"The team had been working on the project since its conception, 
and they were exhausted."

→ Point in time (starting point) + duration effect
```

## 🎯 Summary: How to Avoid Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Solution | Remember |
|---|---|---|
| ❌ "I am liking coffee" | ✓ "I like coffee" | Stative verbs = no Continuous |
| ❌ "I have been writing 5 reports" | ✓ "I have written 5 reports" | Countable = Simple |
| ❌ "The number of students ARE..." | ✓ "The number of students IS..." | THE NUMBER OF = singular |
| ❌ "I have finished yesterday" | ✓ "I finished yesterday" | Specific time = Past Simple |
| ❌ "I worked for 3 hours when he called" | ✓ "I was working for 3 hours when he called" | Duration + interrupted = Continuous |
| ❌ "I am working here for 5 years" | ✓ "I have been working here for 5 years" | Duration to now = Perfect Continuous |
| ❌ "She has worked since morning and is tired" | ✓ "She has been working since morning and is tired" | Since morning (duration) = Continuous |
| ❌ "By the time he arrived, I finished" | ✓ "By the time he arrived, I had finished" | Two past events = Past Perfect |
