# SECTION 2: 3 MỐC THỜI + SIMPLE vs CONTINUOUS — Bước 2 & 3

## 📊 **BẢNG CHÍNH: Simple vs Continuous theo 3 Mốc**

| **Mốc Thời** | **SIMPLE** | **CONTINUOUS** |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **PRESENT** | Thói quen, sự thật (I drink coffee every day) | Đang xảy ra ngay bây giờ (I am drinking coffee) |
| 🟠 **PAST** | Hoàn thành, khoảnh khắc (I finished at 3 PM) | Đang xảy ra + bị ngắt (I was working when you called) |
| 🟣 **PERFECT** (quá khứ → hiện tại) | Kết quả, số lượng (I have written 5 reports) | Kéo dài, nhấn quá trình (I have been working for 3 hours) |

---

## 🔷 **SIGNAL WORDS — Theo Signal Để Chọn Thì**

| **Thì** | **Signal Words** | **Ví Dụ** |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | always, usually, every day, in general | drink coffee every morning |
| **Present Continuous** | now, right now, at the moment, these days | am drinking coffee now |
| **Present Perfect Simple** | just, already, yet, ever, recently, never, so far, this week/month | have just finished |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | for (3 hours), since, how long, all day | have been working for 3 hours |
| **Past Simple** | yesterday, last week, ago, at 5 PM, in 2020 | finished at 3 PM yesterday |
| **Past Continuous** | while, when, as, during, at 3 PM yesterday | was working when you called |
| **Past Perfect Simple** | before, by the time | had finished before he arrived |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | for (duration), how long, before + past | had been working for 2 hours when he came |

---

## 🔷 **FOR vs SINCE vs BY THE TIME — Các Từ Đặc Biệt**

| **Từ** | **Dùng Khi** | **Ví Dụ** |
|---|---|---|
| **FOR** | Khoảng thời gian (3 hours, 2 weeks) | I have worked **for** 3 hours |
| **SINCE** | Điểm bắt đầu (8 AM, 2020, Monday) | I have worked **since** 8 AM |
| **BY THE TIME** | Trước khi sự kiện khác (phải có 2 events) | **By the time** we arrived, they had left |

---

## 💡 **4 GOLDEN RULES — Dùng Cực Nhanh**

1. **For + duration (vẫn đang làm)?** → **Perfect Continuous** (have been -ing)
2. **Specific time (yesterday, at 5 PM)?** → **Past Simple** (-ed)
3. **Hai sự kiện quá khứ, một TRƯỚC cái khác?** → **Past Perfect** (had -ed)
4. **Đếm được kết quả (3 reports, twice)?** → **Perfect Simple** (have -ed, không -ing)

# SECTION 3: SVA TRAPS — Bước 4 (Kiểm Tra Chủ Ngữ)

## 📋 **BẢNG: 6 Tình Huống SVA**

| **Cấu Trúc** | **Qui Tắc** | **SAI ❌** | **ĐÚNG ✅** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The NUMBER of X** | Subject = "The number" (singular) | The number ARE increasing | **The number IS increasing** |
| **A NUMBER of X** | Subject = "A number" (plural) | A number IS coming | **A number ARE coming** |
| **Either A or B** | Verb agree with **NEAREST** noun (B) | Either team or players IS ready | **Either team or players ARE ready** |
| **Neither A nor B** | Verb agree with **NEAREST** noun (B) | Neither she nor I AM wrong | **Neither she nor I ARE wrong** |
| **One of the X** | Subject = "One" (singular) | One of students ARE here | **One of students IS here** |
| **Police** | Always plural (ngoại lệ) | The police IS investigating | **The police ARE investigating** |

---

## 🎯 **MEMORY TIPS — Nhớ Nhanh**

- **THE number** = singular (cái *khái niệm* "số lượng")
- **A number** = plural (nhiều cái)
- **Either/Neither** → động từ agree với từ **GẦN nhất** (sau "or" hoặc "nor")
- **One of** → LUÔN singular (một cái trong số nhiều)
- **Police** → ngoại lệ, LUÔN plural (cũng như people, staff)
